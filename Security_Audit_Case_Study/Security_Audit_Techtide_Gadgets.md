# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Internal Audit Report – TechTide Gadgets

This is a fictional cybersecurity audit report for **TechTide Gadgets**, created as part of my cybersecurity portfolio. It demonstrates knowledge and practical application of internal controls assessment, risk management, and compliance evaluation using the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)**.

---

## 🏢 About the Company (Fictional)

**TechTide Gadgets** is a mid-sized Canadian tech retailer with both physical locations and an international ecommerce platform. As the company expands its operations globally, especially into the U.S. and EU, its IT department faces growing challenges around data protection, system integrity, and regulatory compliance.

To address these concerns, the IT Director initiated an internal audit to assess:

- IT security controls
- Regulatory compliance posture (e.g., **GDPR**, **PCI DSS**)
- Business continuity and disaster recovery preparedness

---

## 🗂️ Audit Scope and Goals

**Scope:**  
The audit encompasses all IT infrastructure, including:

- On-prem and cloud servers  
- Customer data systems  
- Network components  
- Employee workstations and POS systems  
- Physical security of storefronts  

**Goals:**  
- Assess current security controls  
- Identify gaps in compliance  
- Recommend improvements aligned with NIST CSF  

---

## 🔍 Risk Assessment Summary

- **Risk Score:** 9/10 (High)
- **Key Issues Identified:**
  - Unrestricted data access for employees  
  - No encryption for sensitive data  
  - Weak password policy  
  - No formal disaster recovery or backup strategy  

---

## ✅ Controls Assessment

| Status | Control | Explanation |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| ❌ | Least Privilege | All employees have unrestricted access to sensitive systems and customer data. |
| ❌ | Disaster Recovery Plan | No recovery strategy is currently documented. |
| ✅ | Firewall | Enterprise firewall in place and configured. |
| ❓ | Password Policies | Existing policies lack complexity and MFA enforcement. |
| ✅ | Antivirus | Active, updated antivirus on endpoints. |
| ❌ | Backups | Inconsistent local backups, no offsite/cloud solution. |
| ❌ | Encryption | Customer and employee data not encrypted at rest. |
| ❌ | IDS | No intrusion detection system in place. |
| ✅ | Storefront Security | Physical locations have locks and restricted areas. |
| ✅ | CCTV | All stores under video surveillance. |
| ✅ | Fire Detection | Operational alarms in place, but response procedures undocumented. |

## 📋 Compliance Checklist

### PCI DSS

| Status | Best Practice | Explanation |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ | Authorized user access only | Employees access credit card data without restriction. |
| ❌ | Secure card data storage | Credit card data is not encrypted or tokenized. |
| ❌ | Strong encryption in place | No encryption during transmission or storage. |

### GDPR

| Status | Best Practice | Explanation |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ | EU customer data is secured | GDPR principles are not followed for EU customers. |
| ✅ | Privacy policies available | Policy is publicly accessible and regularly updated. |

### SOC (System and Organization Controls)

| Status | Best Practice | Explanation |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ | Access policies enforced | No RBAC or policy governing data access. |
| ✅ | Data integrity maintained | Systems in place to ensure data consistency. |
| ❌ | Controlled data availability | Too many employees can access sensitive data. |

---

## 📌 Recommendations

- Implement **least privilege** access model  
- Develop a **Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)**  
- Enforce **strong password policies** and enable **MFA**  
- Use **encryption** for data at rest and in transit  
- Deploy an **IDS/IPS** solution  
- Ensure **GDPR** and **PCI DSS** compliance  
- Centralize **access control policies** and audit logs
